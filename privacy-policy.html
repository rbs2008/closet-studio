Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Version 3

App Store setup required: Publish this policy at a publicly accessible URL and use that URL in App Store Connect before submitting the app.
The short version: Closet Studio processes your photos on your device and stores your wardrobe locally. To remove photo backgrounds, it downloads a code library from jsDelivr and an AI model from Hugging Face (about 94 MB on supported devices). Those providers may receive normal request metadata, but your photo is not uploaded to them or to Closet Studio.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes the current Closet Studio app, including its wardrobe library, clothing photo processing, weather filters, color matching, and outfit suggestions.

Information stored on your device

When you add a clothing item, the app stores the information needed to show and organize it:

  • The clothing photo you select. The app resizes the image on your device before saving it.
  • The item name, clothing type, color, and weather or season tags you choose.
  • A color estimate sampled from the image, which is processed on your device and can be changed by you.
  • If you choose to create a profile, your name, email address, and profile creation time. This device-only profile has no password, email verification, online sign-in, or cross-device sync.
  • Your temporary temperature entry and generated outfit while the app is open. These are not added to the saved wardrobe.
  • A local record that you accepted this policy, including its version and acceptance time, so the app can remember your choice.

Wardrobe entries, the optional profile, and the acceptance record are stored in local browser storage on the device running the app. They are not uploaded to an online account or a Closet Studio server. Sample wardrobe items may appear when the app has no saved wardrobe data.

Photos and device permissions

You choose which image to add using the file or photo picker provided by your device. The app only processes the image you select. It does not scan your photo library or access other images. Image resizing, background removal, cropping, and color sampling happen on your device. The resulting photo is saved in this app's local browser storage.

How information is used

Information stored in the app is used to display your wardrobe, filter clothing by type and weather, estimate item colors, and create outfit suggestions. Photo segmentation and outfit matching run on your device.

Model and code downloads

The first time you add a photo, the app downloads the Transformers.js code library from jsDelivr and the BiRefNet Lite background-removal model from Hugging Face. The model download is about 94 MB when WebGPU is supported; devices using CPU processing may download a larger model and process more slowly. These downloads require an internet connection. jsDelivr and Hugging Face may receive your IP address, browser or device request headers, and the requested resource as part of ordinary web requests. They do not receive the photo you selected. The app does not send wardrobe photos or item details to these providers.

Sharing, advertising, and tracking

The current version does not transmit wardrobe information to the publisher. It does not include advertising, in-app analytics, or cross-app tracking. Apart from the model and code downloads described above, it does not send photos or wardrobe entries to third parties. The device operating system and app distribution platform may process information under their own privacy terms.

If a future version adds online accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or other data sharing, this policy and the app's privacy disclosures must be updated before those features are used.

Retention and deletion

You can delete individual wardrobe items in the app. Deleting your device-only account removes its name and email as well as all saved wardrobe items and photos from this device. The separate privacy-policy acceptance record remains. You can also clear the app's data using your device or browser settings; this removes the local policy acceptance record and the app will ask you to review the policy again next time. You can open Privacy & consent in the app and decline to withdraw the saved acceptance; the wardrobe remains locked until you agree again.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove locally stored items. The exact behavior depends on how the app is installed and your device settings. Closet Studio cannot recover data deleted from the device.

Security

Closet Studio does not create an online account or transmit your wardrobe to its publisher. The optional local profile is not used to authenticate a login. Local browser storage is not encrypted by Closet Studio itself. Secure your device and user profile, and avoid adding images you do not want stored on that device.

Children

Closet Studio is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or transmit information from the device. A parent or guardian who has a concern can use the contact details below.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be revised and the policy version presented in the app may change. The app will request agreement again when a new policy version requires it.
