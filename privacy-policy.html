Privacy Policy

The short version: Your selected photo is compressed, processed to remove its background, cropped, and analyzed for clothing type and color on this device. HEIC/HEIF photos may require an additional converter download.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes the current Closet Studio app, including its wardrobe library, clothing photo processing, weather filters, color matching, and outfit suggestions.

Information stored on your device

When you add a clothing item, the app stores the information needed to show and organize it:

  • The clothing photo you select. The app resizes the image on your device before saving it.
  • Any previously saved online image links remain on their original websites and are loaded from there when displayed.
  • The item name, clothing type suggested by an on-device image classifier, color, weather or season tags, and occasion tags. You can change the name and type. Occasion tags are suggested from the item name and type when adding a piece.
  • Your favorite outfit selections, which refer to the clothing items saved in your wardrobe and include their names, types, and colors as a local preview.
  • Your appearance preferences, including theme, color palette, and layout spacing.
  • Your app preferences, including default outfit occasion, color-matching style, temperature unit, and wardrobe season filter.
  • A color estimate sampled from the image, which is processed on your device and can be changed by you.
  • If you choose to create a profile, your name, email address, and profile creation time. This device-only profile has no password, email verification, online sign-in, or cross-device sync.
  • Your temporary temperature entry and generated outfit while the app is open. These are not added to the saved wardrobe.
  • A local record that you accepted this policy, including its version and acceptance time, so the app can remember your choice.

Wardrobe entries, the optional profile, photos, and the acceptance record are stored in local browser storage on the device running the app. They are not uploaded to an online account. Previously saved online image links may cause the browser to request an image from its original host, which may receive your IP address and normal request metadata. Sample wardrobe items may appear when the app has no saved wardrobe data.

Photos and device permissions

You choose which image to add using the file or photo picker provided by your device. The app only processes the image you select and does not scan your photo library or access other images. Closet Studio compresses the photo, runs background removal, crops to the clothing, samples its color, and classifies its clothing type on your device. The selected photo is not sent to an image-search service. If background removal is unavailable, the app prepares a compressed copy of the original photo so you can still save it.

How information is used

Information stored in the app is used to display your wardrobe, including any online image link you saved, filter clothing by type and weather, estimate item colors, suggest outfits for the occasion you select, restore outfits you saved as favorites, and apply your chosen appearance preferences. Occasion suggestions and outfit matching run on your device.

Model and code downloads

On first use, the app may download the Transformers.js code library and, if a HEIC/HEIF photo needs conversion, the heic2any converter from jsDelivr. It may also download the BiRefNet Lite background-removal model and a small MobileNet clothing-type classifier from Hugging Face. These downloads require an internet connection and may use data; the providers receive ordinary download request information but not the selected photo. Conversion and model analysis happen locally on the device. Background removal can take longer on phones; if it cannot complete, the app offers a compressed original instead.

Sharing, advertising, and tracking

The app does not include advertising, in-app analytics, or cross-app tracking. Closet Studio does not upload your wardrobe, profile, or newly selected clothing photos. Image resizing, background removal, cropping, color sampling, and clothing-type classification happen on your device. Previously saved online image links may still contact their original image hosts when displayed. The device operating system and app distribution platform may process information under their own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

You can delete individual wardrobe items in the app; favorite outfits that include a deleted item are also removed. Deleting your device-only account removes its name and email as well as all saved wardrobe items, photos, and favorite outfits from this device. The separate privacy-policy acceptance record remains. You can also clear the app's data using your device or browser settings; this removes the local policy acceptance record and the app will ask you to review the policy again next time. You can open Privacy & consent in the app and decline to withdraw the saved acceptance; the wardrobe remains locked until you agree again.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove locally stored items. The exact behavior depends on how the app is installed and your device settings. Closet Studio cannot recover data deleted from the device.

The Data settings let you export a local backup file or import one. A backup may include clothing photos and optional profile information; keep it private and delete downloaded backups separately when they are no longer needed. Import replaces the current local wardrobe and settings. Closet Studio does not upload backup files. Deleting all local data leaves the separate policy-acceptance record in place.

Security

Closet Studio does not create an online account or sync your wardrobe. The optional local profile is not used to authenticate a login. Local browser storage is not encrypted by Closet Studio itself. Secure your device and user profile. Photos selected for new wardrobe items are processed locally and are not sent to an image-search provider.

Children

Closet Studio is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Photos selected for clothing items are processed on the device and are not sent to an image-search service. A parent or guardian who has a concern can use the contact details below.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be revised and the policy version presented in the app may change. The app will request agreement again when a new policy version requires it.
