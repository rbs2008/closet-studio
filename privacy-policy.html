Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026 · Version 10

App Store setup required: Publish this policy at a publicly accessible URL and use that URL in App Store Connect before submitting the app.
The short version: Your selected photo stays on this device unless you tap the online image-search button. That sends a resized copy through the Closet Studio search service to Google Cloud Vision Web Detection. Search results are suggestions; the selected image URL and item details are saved locally. Google and Cloudflare process requests under their own privacy terms.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes the current Closet Studio app, including its wardrobe library, clothing photo processing, weather filters, color matching, and outfit suggestions.

Information stored on your device

When you add a clothing item, the app stores the information needed to show and organize it:

  • The clothing photo you select. The app resizes the image on your device before saving it.
  • If you choose an online image, its URL and the item title you enter. The URL is saved locally, while the image itself remains on its original website and is loaded from there when displayed.
  • The item name, clothing type, color, weather or season tags, and occasion tags. Occasion tags are suggested from the item name and type when adding a piece and can be changed by you.
  • Your favorite outfit selections, which refer to the clothing items saved in your wardrobe and include their names, types, and colors as a local preview.
  • Your appearance preferences, including theme, color palette, and layout spacing.
  • Your app preferences, including default outfit occasion, color-matching style, temperature unit, and wardrobe season filter.
  • A color estimate sampled from the image, which is processed on your device and can be changed by you.
  • If you choose to create a profile, your name, email address, and profile creation time. This device-only profile has no password, email verification, online sign-in, or cross-device sync.
  • Your temporary temperature entry and generated outfit while the app is open. These are not added to the saved wardrobe.
  • A local record that you accepted this policy, including its version and acceptance time, so the app can remember your choice.

Wardrobe entries, the optional profile, and the acceptance record are stored in local browser storage on the device running the app. They are not uploaded to an online account. If you choose online image search, the selected photo is sent to Google Cloud Vision through a Closet Studio Cloudflare Worker for that search; the app does not intentionally store the photo on the Worker. Google and Cloudflare may receive request information under their own terms. When an online image link is displayed, the browser requests it from its host, which may receive your IP address and normal request metadata. Sample wardrobe items may appear when the app has no saved wardrobe data.

Photos and device permissions

You choose which image to add using the file or photo picker provided by your device. The app only processes the image you select and does not scan your photo library or access other images. Image resizing and color sampling happen on your device. On phones, the app does not run background removal. If you tap Find an online photo and title, a resized JPEG copy of the selected image is sent to the Closet Studio search Worker, which forwards it to Google Cloud Vision Web Detection and returns possible image links and page titles. The app does not intentionally log or store the photo on the Worker; Google and Cloudflare process the request under their own privacy terms. This search is optional. If you do not use it, your uploaded photo remains in this app's local browser storage. The separate Google Lens link opens Lens for you to use directly.

How information is used

Information stored in the app is used to display your wardrobe, including any online image link you saved, filter clothing by type and weather, estimate item colors, suggest outfits for the occasion you select, restore outfits you saved as favorites, and apply your chosen appearance preferences. Occasion suggestions and outfit matching run on your device.

Model and code downloads

Desktop background removal may download the Transformers.js code library from jsDelivr and the BiRefNet Lite model from Hugging Face. These downloads require an internet connection. jsDelivr and Hugging Face may receive your IP address, browser or device request headers, and the requested resource as part of ordinary web requests; they do not receive the photo selected for background removal. Phone uploads skip this model. Online image search is a separate, optional feature that sends a resized photo to Google Cloud Vision through the search Worker as described above.

Sharing, advertising, and tracking

The app does not include advertising, in-app analytics, or cross-app tracking. Closet Studio does not upload your wardrobe or profile. If you tap the online-search button, the selected resized photo is sent to the Closet Studio Cloudflare Worker and Google Cloud Vision Web Detection to find possible matches. The Worker relays the request and does not intentionally store the photo; Google and Cloudflare may process it under their own terms. The item you select, its title, and the image URL are saved locally. Loading a saved online image sends ordinary request metadata to the image host. The device operating system and app distribution platform may process information under their own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

You can delete individual wardrobe items in the app; favorite outfits that include a deleted item are also removed. Deleting your device-only account removes its name and email as well as all saved wardrobe items, photos, and favorite outfits from this device. The separate privacy-policy acceptance record remains. You can also clear the app's data using your device or browser settings; this removes the local policy acceptance record and the app will ask you to review the policy again next time. You can open Privacy & consent in the app and decline to withdraw the saved acceptance; the wardrobe remains locked until you agree again.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove locally stored items. The exact behavior depends on how the app is installed and your device settings. Closet Studio cannot recover data deleted from the device.

The Data settings let you export a local backup file or import one. A backup may include clothing photos and optional profile information; keep it private and delete downloaded backups separately when they are no longer needed. Import replaces the current local wardrobe and settings. Closet Studio does not upload backup files. Deleting all local data leaves the separate policy-acceptance record in place.

Security

Closet Studio does not create an online account or sync your wardrobe. The optional local profile is not used to authenticate a login. Local browser storage is not encrypted by Closet Studio itself. Secure your device and user profile. Do not use online image search for photos you do not want sent to Google Cloud Vision and processed by Google and Cloudflare.

Children

Closet Studio is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If online image search is used, the selected photo is sent to Google Cloud Vision and processed by Google and Cloudflare. A parent or guardian who has a concern can use the contact details below.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be revised and the policy version presented in the app may change. The app will request agreement again when a new policy version requires it.
